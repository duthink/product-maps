# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Product MAPS is an open, collaborative project.
It exists to help product teams think more clearly about what they build,
for whom, and why.

To do that well, we need a space where people can:
- ask naïve questions
- challenge assumptions
- disagree respectfully
- share unfinished thinking

This Code of Conduct sets the expectations for how we work together.

---

## Our Values

We value:

- **Respectful disagreement**  
  Good ideas improve through challenge, not dominance.

- **Curiosity over certainty**  
  Questions are more valuable than confident opinions.

- **Clarity over cleverness**  
  Clear thinking matters more than sounding smart.

- **Real experience over abstraction**  
  Lived product work beats theory every time.

---

## Expected Behavior

We expect all contributors and participants to:

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Listen before responding
- Critique ideas, not people
- Assume good intent, especially when things are unclear
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Dismissing others based on role, experience level, or background
- Derailing discussions for ego, ideology, or self-promotion
- Repeatedly ignoring feedback or community guidance
- Using the project as a platform for sales or unsolicited promotion

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Documentation
- Any official communication channels related to Product MAPS

---

## Final Note

Product MAPS is about navigating complexity together.

That requires trust, patience, and humility.

We expect everyone here — maintainers included —
to keep learning, adjusting, and improving how we collaborate.
