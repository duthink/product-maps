# Product Map — Hotel Booking Drop-Off (Travel / Hospitality)

This Product Map documents how the team addressed
high search activity but low booking completion
on a hotel booking platform.

Users were browsing.
Rooms were available.
But bookings were not converting.

---

## 1. What are we trying to move from → to?
(M — Mark)

**From**
- High search and room view traffic
- Significant drop-off before payment
- Frequent “checking availability” without booking

**To**
- Confident booking completion
- Reduced abandonment at final steps
- Fewer post-search hesitations

**Who experiences this change the most**
- Leisure travelers booking short stays
- First-time users unfamiliar with the property
- Mobile users booking close to travel dates

---

## 2. What do we believe — and what do we know?
(M → A)

**What we believed to be true**
- Users are price-sensitive
- Lower prices or discounts will increase bookings
- Faster checkout will improve conversion

**What evidence supported this**
- Price comparison behavior across platforms
- Drop-offs near the final payment step
- Competitive pressure from OTAs

**What felt uncertain or risky**
- Whether users trusted property details
- Whether policies were clear enough at booking time
- Whether urgency helped or hurt confidence

---

## 3. What does reality push back on?
(A — Align)

**How travelers actually behave**
- Compare reviews and photos across platforms
- Re-check cancellation and refund policies
- Pause to confirm dates, check with companions, or verify location

**Constraints that became clear**
- Travel is a high-anxiety purchase
- Plans often change
- Users seek reassurance more than speed

**What changed after looking at reality**
- Price was not the primary blocker
- Uncertainty and risk perception caused hesitation
- Lack of clarity increased drop-offs

---

## 4. What is the next meaningful step?
(P — Proceed)

**Purpose of the next milestone**
- Reduce booking anxiety at the decision moment

**What we deliberately did not do**
- Blanket discounts
- Artificial scarcity messages
- Major UI redesign

**Smallest step that proves direction**
- Surface cancellation policy clearly before payment
- Highlight verified guest reviews near price
- Show exact location context (map + landmarks)

**How we’ll know this worked**
- Increased payment-step completion
- Reduced time spent hesitating before booking
- Fewer booking-related support queries

---

## 5. How will we know if we’re wrong?
(S — Sync)

**Signals we’ll monitor**
- Step-level conversion rates
- Time spent on booking confirmation page
- Support tickets tagged “booking clarity”

**Review cadence**
- Daily for first week
- Weekly thereafter

**Reroute triggers**
- Conversion does not improve after 10 days
- Support tickets remain unchanged
- Users still abandon after viewing policies
