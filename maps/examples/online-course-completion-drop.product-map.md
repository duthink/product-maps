# Product Map — Online Course Completion Drop (EdTech)

This Product Map documents how the team addressed
low completion rates in an online learning platform.

Enrollments were high.
Content quality was strong.
But most learners were dropping out midway.

---

## 1. What are we trying to move from → to?
(M — Mark)

**From**
- High course enrollment but low completion rates
- Learners dropping out after the first few modules
- Instructors questioning learner motivation

**To**
- Meaningful course completion
- Learners reaching learning outcomes
- Reduced drop-off after initial enthusiasm

**Who experiences this change the most**
- First-time learners
- Working professionals balancing study with jobs
- Instructors tracking learner progress

---

## 2. What do we believe — and what do we know?
(M → A)

**What we believed to be true**
- Learners want comprehensive, in-depth content
- More lessons equal more value
- Drop-offs are due to lack of discipline

**What evidence supported this**
- Positive feedback on early modules
- High watch time on introductory videos
- Instructor confidence in course depth

**What felt uncertain or risky**
- Whether learners could sustain long study sessions
- Whether course pacing matched real-life schedules
- Whether learners understood progress expectations

---

## 3. What does reality push back on?
(A — Align)

**How learners actually behave**
- Study in short bursts between other commitments
- Pause courses for days or weeks
- Return only if progress feels achievable

**Constraints that became clear**
- Attention and energy are limited resources
- Learners measure progress emotionally, not academically
- Long gaps reduce confidence to resume

**What changed after looking at reality**
- Course length was intimidating
- Learners needed visible progress milestones
- Completion felt distant, not motivating

---

## 4. What is the next meaningful step?
(P — Proceed)

**Purpose of the next milestone**
- Help learners feel early momentum and confidence

**What we deliberately did not do**
- Rewrite all course content
- Reduce academic rigor
- Add more reminders or notifications

**Smallest step that proves direction**
- Break courses into short learning paths
- Introduce clear “first win” milestones
- Show progress visually after each short module

**How we’ll know this worked**
- Increased completion of early modules
- Higher return rates after breaks
- Positive learner feedback on pacing

---

## 5. How will we know if we’re wrong?
(S — Sync)

**Signals we’ll monitor**
- Module-to-module completion rate
- Time gaps between sessions
- Learner feedback on course length and clarity

**Review cadence**
- Weekly for first month

**Reroute triggers**
- Early completion does not improve
- Learners still drop off after initial modules
- Feedback shows confusion rather than confidence

---
