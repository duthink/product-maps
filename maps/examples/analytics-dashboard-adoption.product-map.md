# Product Map — Analytics Dashboard Adoption

This Product Map captures how the team navigated
low adoption of a newly launched analytics dashboard.

The dashboard was technically sound but underused.
This map documents how assumptions were revisited
and direction adjusted.

---

## 1. What are we trying to move from → to?
(M — Mark)

**From**
- A newly launched analytics dashboard
- Low daily active usage despite strong internal confidence
- Repeated internal explanations of “users just need time”

**To**
- Regular usage by decision-makers
- Dashboards becoming part of weekly workflows
- Reduced ad-hoc data requests to the team

**Who experiences this change the most**
- Operations managers
- Team leads responsible for weekly reporting

---

## 2. What do we believe — and what do we know?
(M → A)

**What we believed to be true**
- Users want more visibility into their data
- A single dashboard would replace manual reporting
- Better charts lead to better decisions

**What evidence supported this**
- Frequent requests for reports
- Positive feedback during internal demos
- Strong feature parity with competitor dashboards

**What felt uncertain**
- Whether users had time to interpret dashboards
- Whether insights aligned with how decisions were actually made

---

## 3. What does reality push back on?
(A — Align)

**How users handled this today**
- Asked for weekly summaries via email
- Took screenshots and shared them in chats
- Used only one or two metrics, ignoring the rest

**Constraints that became clear**
- Users had limited time during their workday
- Dashboards required interpretation effort
- Decisions were often made in meetings, not tools

**What changed after looking at reality**
- The dashboard was competing with existing habits
- Users wanted conclusions, not just visibility

---

## 4. What is the next meaningful step?
(P — Proceed)

**Purpose of the next milestone**
- Test whether contextual insights improve usage
  more than adding new charts

**What we deliberately stopped doing**
- Adding more metrics
- Improving visual polish without changing workflow fit

**Smallest step that proves direction**
- Introduce a weekly automated insight summary
- Highlight only 3 metrics tied to decisions

**How we’ll know this worked**
- Increase in weekly engagement
- Reduction in manual reporting requests

---

## 5. How will we know if we’re wrong?
(S — Sync)

**Signals we’ll monitor**
- Weekly active users (not daily)
- Click-through from insight summaries
- Feedback in review meetings

**Review cadence**
- Bi-weekly for six weeks

**Reroute triggers**
- Users ignore summaries
- Requests for manual reports remain unchanged
- Engagement drops after initial curiosity

---

## Notes / Revisions

- 2025-02-03 — Shifted focus from dashboards to insights  
  Reason: user interviews revealed decision-making happens outside tools
