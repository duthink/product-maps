# Product Map — Self-Serve Onboarding

This Product Map documents how the team is navigating
the move from manual onboarding to self-serve onboarding.

It reflects current understanding and will be updated
as reality changes.

---

## 1. What are we trying to move from → to?
(M — Mark)

**From**
- Manual onboarding handled by sales and support
- 2–3 day turnaround for new customers
- High effort for internal teams

**To**
- Self-serve onboarding completed in under 5 minutes
- Minimal human involvement for standard use cases

**Who experiences this change the most**
- New SMB customers signing up without sales assistance
- Support team currently handling onboarding tickets

---

## 2. What do we believe — and what do we know?
(M → A)

**What we believe to be true**
- Faster onboarding will improve activation
- Users prefer not to talk to sales for simple setups
- Clear guidance reduces support load

**What evidence supports this**
- Support tickets show repeated onboarding questions
- Funnel data shows drop-off before first successful action
- Sales reports indicate many users only need basic setup

**What feels uncertain or risky**
- Whether users trust self-serve flows for initial setup
- Whether configuration complexity will overwhelm first-time users

---

## 3. What does reality push back on?
(A — Align)

**How users handle this today**
- Schedule calls with support
- Ask for step-by-step email instructions
- Delay setup until they “have time”

**Constraints that won’t bend**
- Some configurations require domain access
- Users vary widely in technical confidence
- Compliance requires certain steps not be skipped

**What changed after looking at reality**
- Fully automated onboarding may not work for all users
- A guided, assisted self-serve flow may be more realistic

---

## 4. What is the next meaningful step?
(P — Proceed)

**Purpose of the next milestone**
- Validate whether guided self-serve onboarding improves activation
  without increasing support load

**What we are deliberately not doing**
- Full automation for advanced configurations
- Redesigning the entire signup flow

**Smallest step that proves direction**
- Introduce a guided onboarding checklist
- Enable optional “request help” at each step

**How we’ll know this worked**
- Activation rate improves for SMB segment
- Support tickets do not increase for onboarding issues

---

## 5. How will we know if we’re wrong?
(S — Sync)

**Signals we’ll monitor**
- Week-1 activation rate
- Drop-off per onboarding step
- Onboarding-related support tickets

**Review cadence**
- Weekly review for first 4 weeks

**Reroute triggers**
- Activation does not improve after 2 weeks
- Support tickets increase by more than X%
- Users abandon onboarding at the same step repeatedly
