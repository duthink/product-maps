# Product Map Template (Canonical)

> This is the canonical Product Map template used by Product MAPS. All examples and future variants should derive from this structure.

**Product MAPS** is the framework. **MAPS** is the four-step loop inside it (Mark, Align, Proceed, Sync).

This Product Map captures how a team is navigating a product decision at a specific point in time.

It is a **living document**.
Revisit it whenever assumptions, constraints, or signals change.

You do not need perfect answers.
You need shared understanding.

## Mark — Your position
*Where are we actually starting from?*

This section clarifies the current state before solutions are debated.

**1) The problem as we understand it**
- What problem are we trying to solve?
- For whom does this problem matter most?
- Why does it matter now?

**2) Assumptions we are making**
- What do we believe to be true about users, behavior, or context?
- Which of these are untested?
- Which assumptions would be most damaging if wrong?

**3) How we define success**
- What outcome would tell us this worked?
- Who benefits if this succeeds?
- What does “good enough” look like?

---

## Align — With reality
*What does the world push back on?*

This section pressure-tests assumptions against constraints and signals.

**4) What we see in reality**
- How do users behave today?
- What workarounds or habits already exist?
- What signals do we have (qualitative or quantitative)?

**5) Constraints we must respect**
- Technical constraints
- Operational constraints
- Cultural or behavioral constraints
- Time, budget, or regulatory constraints

**6) What changed after looking at reality**
- Which assumptions weakened?
- Which became stronger?
- What surprised us?

---

## Proceed — With purpose
*What is the next meaningful step?*

This section turns insight into intentional action.

**7) Purpose of the next step**
- What are we trying to unlock or learn next?
- Why is this the right step now?

**8) What we are deliberately not doing**
- What are we postponing or excluding?
- What distractions are we filtering out?

**9) Smallest step that proves direction**
- What is the thinnest slice that still creates signal?
- What change does this ask from users or teams?

---

## Sync — With feedback
*How will we adapt if things change?*

This section defines how learning feeds back into decisions.

**10) Signals we will monitor**
- Which metrics matter?
- Which qualitative signals matter?
- What would indicate progress vs noise?

**11) Review cadence**
- How often will we review this?
- Who participates in the review?

**12) Reroute triggers**
- What would tell us this is not working?
- What conditions force us to rethink direction?
